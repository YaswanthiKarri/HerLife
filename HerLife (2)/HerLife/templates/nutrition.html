🔴 Higher-Risk Nutrition Plan

Your screening result shows a higher-risk category for PCOD and/or PCOS. This plan focuses on balanced meals with vegetables, protein, fiber-rich carbohydrates and moderate portions.

🌅 Early Morning

  • 💧 Warm water
  • 🥜 5–6 soaked almonds or a small handful of nuts
  • 🍎 Optional: guava, apple or orange

🥣 Breakfast

  • 🌱 Pesarattu with vegetable chutney
  • 🥣 Vegetable oats upma
  • 🌾 Ragi dosa with sambar
  • 🍲 Idli with vegetable-rich sambar
  • 🥚 Egg with vegetables, if you eat eggs

🍊 Mid-Morning

  • 🍎 One whole fruit
  • 🍊 Orange / guava / apple / papaya
  • 💧 Water

☀️ Lunch

  • 🍚 Moderate portion of rice
  • 🫘 Dal / pappu
  • 🥬 Two vegetable dishes
  • 🥣 Sambar or rasam
  • 🥗 Cucumber/carrot/onion salad
  • 🥛 Plain curd
  • 🐟 Fish or chicken can be included if preferred

🍵 Evening Snack

  • 🫘 Roasted chana
  • 🌱 Sprouts or sundal
  • 🥛 Unsweetened buttermilk
  • 🥜 Small portion of peanuts or nuts
  • 🍵 Unsweetened tea

🌙 Dinner

  • 🥣 Vegetable soup with dal
  • 🫓 Jowar roti with vegetable curry
  • 🌾 Ragi dosa with sambar
  • 🍲 Vegetable millet khichdi
  • 🥬 Dal with plenty of vegetables
⚠️ Try to limit frequent:
  • 🥤 Sugary soft drinks
  • 🧃 Frequent packaged fruit drinks
  • 🍰 Large amounts of sweets
  • 🍪 Frequent biscuits and bakery foods
  • 🍟 Deep-fried snacks
  • 🍚 Very large portions of refined carbohydrates

💧 Daily Healthy Habits

⚠️ Important

This nutrition information is for general wellness and educational purposes. A PCOD/PCOS screening result is not a diagnosis.

There is no single food or diet that cures PCOD or PCOS. Individual nutrition needs can vary, so personalized advice from a qualified healthcare professional or dietitian may be appropriate.
