🏃 Why Exercise?

Regular physical activity can support fitness, energy, mood and overall health.

Choose activities that are comfortable for your fitness level and increase activity gradually.

🚶 Brisk Walking

15–30 minutes

Walk at a comfortable but slightly faster pace.

Instructions

  • Keep your back straight.
  • Move your arms naturally.
  • Start slowly and increase your pace.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Take breaks when needed.

🚴 Comfortable Cycling

20–30 minutes

Cycling can be a useful cardiovascular activity.

Tips

  • Start at an easy pace.
  • Wear a helmet.
  • Keep a comfortable posture.
  • Drink water regularly.
  • Take breaks when needed.

🌸 Exercise & PCOD/PCOS Wellness

Regular physical activity may support overall metabolic and cardiovascular health for people with PCOS.

  • 🚶 Walking
  • 🏋️ Strength exercises
  • 🧘 Yoga
  • 🚴 Cycling
  • 💃 Dancing
  • 🤸 Gentle stretching

📅 Simple Weekly Plan

  • Monday: 🚶 Walking
  • Tuesday: 🏋️ Strength exercises
  • Wednesday: 🧘 Yoga / Stretching
  • Thursday: 🚶 Walking
  • Friday: 🏋️ Strength exercises
  • Saturday: 💃 Dancing / Cycling
  • Sunday: 🧘 Rest or gentle stretching

⚠️ Exercise Safety

Stop exercising if you feel severe pain, dizziness, fainting, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath.

Choose exercises appropriate for your fitness level and increase activity gradually.
